Vuori · 58/100 · audited 14–15 Oct 2026

Agents know Vuori and like it. Vuori doesn't control what they say.

27 shopper questions and 3 purchase tasks across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, plus a technical read of 4 hero products: Sunday Performance Jogger, Kore Short, Women's Performance Jogger, Meta Pant.

Where Vuori stands

  1. Known, not controlled.Recommended in 22 of 27 shopper questions, but Vuori's own site was a source in 1 of 24. Agents learn from magazines and Reddit, and some facts come back wrong.
    KPI · Agent Sessions
  2. The feed undersells the product.ChatGPT shows the Sunday Jogger with 3,547 reviews; Vuori's site shows 14,596.
    KPI · Conversion
  3. The buy step fails.Asked to buy one exact Sunday variant, 0 of 3 agents gave a checkout-ready link.
    KPI · Attributed Revenue

Fix first, next 30 days

  1. Agent-ready listings for the 4 hero products.Who it's for, top use cases, fit, and when not to buy. Low effort.
  2. Fix the product feed.Group-level review totals and links to the exact variant. Low to medium effort.
  3. Add structured attributes.Inseam, lining, fit, shipping and returns as data, not only in copy. Medium effort.

GlassesUSA · 53/100 · audited 17 Oct 2026

Agents name GlassesUSA as the place to buy. They don't sell its frames.

The same 27 + 3 runs, plus a bot-access test with 13 crawler user agents. Hero products: Oakley Sutro (running), Muse Elliot (house brand), Ottoto Ellsworth (blue-light), Ray-Ban RB2140 Wayfarer.

Where GlassesUSA stands

  1. A store, not a product.Named in 9 of 9 "where to buy prescription glasses" answers, but 0 of 9 running-sunglasses and 0 of 3 blue-light answers.
    KPI · Agent Sessions
  2. The real price is invisible.An Rx Wayfarer costs $215.50 with lenses, but that total sits inside a 4-step configurator. 0 of 6 agent answers stated it.
    KPI · Conversion
  3. The buy step breaks.Asked to buy one exact Wayfarer, 2 of 3 agents linked a duplicate listing at a different price.
    KPI · Attributed Revenue

Fix first, next 30 days

  1. Publish the complete price and Rx status."With single-vision lenses from $X" and "prescription eligible" as text and schema on hero pages. Medium effort.
  2. Agent-ready listings and a running FAQ that names products.Say who each hero product is for. Low effort.
  3. Let verified AI agents through bot protection.About 1 in 5 non-JavaScript page requests got an empty challenge page. Low to medium effort.

Method and tools

AI collected and drafted. I checked every claim and made the calls.

One method for both brands, so the two scores compare. Vuori built the method; GlassesUSA reused it with almost all data collection automated.

  1. FramingBrief, 40rty's product and its 4 KPIs. Every finding names one.
  2. Hero products4 per brand, each tied to one shopper prompt.
  3. Technical readPlatform, crawler access, raw vs rendered HTML, schema, policies, guest checkout.
  4. AI visibility5 prompts × 3 engines, main 2 run 3 times, plus 3 purchase tasks.
  5. VerificationEach engine claim checked on the brand's site: confirmed, error or open.
  6. Score and planScripted metrics, weighted score, 6 ranked fixes with owners.
ToolHow I used it
Claude (Opus, Fable)Main analyst and engineer: parsed raw HTML and JSON-LD, wrote the scripts, drafted findings, built the decks and emails.
Claude in ChromeBrowser agent that ran every prompt in a new temporary chat, saved screenshots and full text, and logged one CSV row per run.
GrokIndependent second auditor on GlassesUSA. Its claims were re-checked on the live site before any were accepted.
Python and PowerShellMetrics computed from the run log, never typed by hand. Crawler test: 13 user agents × 7 pages, run twice.
US VPN, clean profileNew accounts, English UI, default free models, web search on, location checked before each batch.
ChatGPT, Perplexity, GeminiThe subject of the audit, tested only as a shopper sees them.
What checking caught. Our own automation labelled Gemini's "Vuori Cascade Tech Chino" a hallucination; the product exists, so Gemini was right. It also read a feed rating of 2,254 reviews as the page rating of 26,547, which became a finding in its own right.
